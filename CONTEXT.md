# Finnova Next Best Action

Retail banking advisor platform evaluating customer context to recommend personalized next best actions.

## Language

**Individual**:
A retail banking customer with financial profiles, attributes, and life situation records.
_Avoid_: User, person, client, account

**Next Best Action**:
A single prioritized advisory recommendation or product offer for an individual based on financial context.
_Avoid_: Recommendation, suggestion, task, offer

**Individual State**:
A comprehensive snapshot of an individual's demographic, behavioral, and risk attributes at a given period.
_Avoid_: Profile, user data, customer state

**Advisor Interaction**:
A past or ongoing engagement between a bank advisor and an individual across specific communication channels.
_Avoid_: Communication, message, ticket

**Account Balance**:
The recorded monetary balance of a financial account belonging to an individual at a specific period.
_Avoid_: Funds, money, account amount
